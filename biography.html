The Journey of a Maestro

From Mthatha to the National Stage

Quick Facts

Origin: Mthatha, Eastern Cape

Instruments: Bass (Electric & Double), Piano, Guitar, Saxophone, Flute

Education: Nelson Mandela University

Key Award: SAMA Nomination (Best Jazz Album)

Early Life & Musical Awakening

Temba Ncetani's story begins in the vibrant city of Mthatha, Eastern Cape. It was here, at a young age, that his profound love for music first manifested. Unlike many musicians who follow formal classical training, Ncetani is a completely self-taught multi-instrumentalist and composer.

His natural talent and dedication allowed him to master a staggering array of instruments. He is equally comfortable playing the electric and double bass, the piano, guitar, saxophone, and flute, making him one of the most versatile musicians in the Eastern Cape. He credits the legendary double bassist Herbie Tsoaeli as his biggest influence, a guiding light in his musical journey.

Academic Journey & Early Career

Driven by his passion, Ncetani pursued formal studies at Nelson Mandela University, where he graduated with a degree in music. Even as a student, he was already making waves in the professional scene. By 2018, he was performing professionally throughout the Bay, showcasing his talents at prestigious events like the National Arts Festival.

From 2010 to 2013, he played bass for the popular jazz band, **Take Note**, contributing to their album *Rear Window* under Native Rhythms. This experience grounded him in the rich tradition of South African jazz and prepared him for the national stage.

Sharing the Stage with Legends

As his reputation grew, Ncetani found himself sharing the stage with some of South Africa's most prominent acts. He has performed alongside Sibongile Khumalo, Vusi Nova, Jimmy Dludlu, and Nomfusi, proving his ability to hold his own among the country's finest.

The Solo Artist: A Tribute to Heritage

While Ncetani is a gifted performer, it is as a composer and solo artist that his true artistry shines. His music is a bridge across genres—fluidly moving between jazz, gospel, reggae, and pop—but it remains deeply rooted in a tribute to Eastern Cape jazz heritage.

"His debut album, INTLUNGU. INTLUPHEKO. IMBILINI., was nominated for Best Jazz Album at the South African Music Awards (SAMAs)."

Released in 2025, his debut album *INTLUNGU. INTLUPHEKO. IMBILINI.* is a masterpiece of atmospheric, emotionally resonant jazz. The album pays homage to various Eastern Cape jazz icons, featuring tracks like *eMthatha (My Hometown)* and *A Song For Faizel (I Remember Siya Mdebele)*. The record's critical acclaim earned him a SAMA nomination, cementing his place as one of the province's finest musical exports.

His commitment to honoring his roots is further evident in his live performances, such as his "Dirge For Eastern Cape Composers" showcase, where he pays tribute to legends like Dudu Pukwana, Duke Makasi, and Dudley 'Bra Dates' Tito.

The Educator: Nurturing the Next Generation

Despite his national success, Temba Ncetani has never forgotten his community. He is a dedicated music educator, deeply committed to nurturing the next generation of South African talent.

He has taught music at **Khanyisa High School** in Mthatha, where he is known simply as "Sir" by his students, and at the online music school, **We See Musically**. His dual career as a SAMA-nominated artist and a high school teacher is a powerful and inspiring story that sets him apart, proving that true success lies in giving back.

Legacy

Temba Ncetani is more than just a musician. He is a composer, an arranger, a mentor, and a cultural ambassador. His journey from a self-taught boy in Mthatha to a SAMA-nominated jazz artist is a testament to the power of passion, hard work, and an unwavering commitment to one's roots. He continues to bridge the gap between the rich jazz heritage of the Eastern Cape and the modern soundscapes of South Africa, letting his light shine for all to see.
