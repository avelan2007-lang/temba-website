The Journey of a Maestro

Rooted in Heritage, Driven by Music & Purpose

Major Nominations:
- SAMAs (Best Jazz Album & Newcomer)

Sonic Identity

Temba Ncetani is a South African jazz musician and educator from Mthatha in the Eastern Cape. His artistic vision centers on blending the sophistication of contemporary jazz with the profound rhythmic and melodic languages of indigenous Xhosa musical expression. This fusion creates a sound that is both global in its technical reach and deeply localized in its soulful essence.

The Journey

Hailing from the Eastern Cape, Temba's journey began as a self-taught musician, driven by an innate connection to harmony. He is a multi-instrumental force proficient in Bass, Piano, Guitar, Saxophone, and Flute, allowing for diverse compositional textures. His work bridges the gap between urban jazz scenes and the heritage-rich landscapes of Mthatha and surrounding regions.

Collaborative Excellence

He has shared stages and collaborated with some of South Africa's finest artists, exploring intricate musical dialogues and arrangements.

  • Jimmy Dludlu: Exploring intricate guitar-led jazz dialogues.
  • Sibongile Khumalo: Accompanying South Africa's 'First Lady of Song'.
  • Vusi Nova: Collaborating on contemporary Afro-soul arrangements.
  • Nomfusi: Bringing vibrant energy to international stage productions.
  • Putuma Tiso: Crafting deep spiritual and gospel-infused jazz.

Major Festivals & Platforms

  • Joy of Jazz Festival: South Africa's premier jazz gathering.
  • National Arts Festival: Makhanda's cultural epicenter.
  • UGU Jazz Festival: Showcasing regional talent at a high level.
  • SABC: Television appearances and FM radio broadcasts.

Debut Album: INTLUNGU. INTLUPHEKO. IMBILINI.

Released in April 2025, this 12-track project reflects his upbringing, influences, and personal journey through sound. It is a sonic exploration translating lived experience into timeless improvisation. Featured tracks include:

  • eMthatha: A soulful homage to the artist's hometown and rhythmic roots.
  • A View From Panorama: Cinematic jazz texture composed for Miss N Fazzie.
  • Avenue A: In honor of the late Dudley 'Bra Dates' Tito; urban and classic.
  • NomaIndia: A deeply personal dedication to his mother, Mrs. NM Ncetani.
"This record's critical acclaim earned him three major nominations: two SAMAs (Best Jazz Album and Newcomer of the Year), a BASA award nomination for Most Innovative Artist, and a UMA nomination for Best Jazz Act."

Off-Stage Impact

Cultural Curator: As Curator for the Mandela Bay Jazz Festival, he contributed to the visibility and availability of jazz in the Nelson Mandela Bay region, bridging the gap between local talent and established jazz giants.

Music Director: At the Village Vanguard Academy, he leads music education, mentorship, and artist development programs, focusing on creating sustainable career paths for emerging South African artists.

Education & Pedagogy

Beyond the stage, Temba serves as a Music Teacher at Khanyisa High School in Mthatha. He is passionate about vibrating musicality through education, ensuring that the history and theory of jazz are accessible to the youth. His approach combines academic rigor with the freedom of improvisational thought.
