More Than Just a Musician

To the students at Khanyisa High School in Mthatha, he is simply "Sir." As a dedicated music educator, Temba Ncetani has dedicated his life to nurturing the next generation of South African talent.

He is a self-taught multi-instrumentalist, a graduate of Nelson Mandela University, and a firm believer that music is the ultimate tool for social change.

His latest album, INTLUNGU. INTLUPHEKO. IMBILINI., earned a SAMA nomination for Best Jazz Album, cementing his place as one of the Eastern Cape's finest musical exports.
