A Distinctive African Jazz Voice

Hailing from Mthatha in the Eastern Cape, Temba Ncetani is a multi-instrumental force proficient in Bass, Piano, Guitar, Saxophone, and Flute. His artistic vision centers on creating a sound that is both global in its technical reach and deeply localized in its soulful essence.

As a Cultural Curator for the Mandela Bay Jazz Festival and Music Director at the Village Vanguard Academy, he is dedicated to bridging the gap between local talent and established jazz giants.
